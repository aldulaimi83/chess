How to Play

Drive clean. Think ahead.

  • Use the car's momentum to climb hills and clear gaps.
  • Tap F to throw the yo-yo at switches, fuel cans, bridge anchors, and light obstacles.
  • Tap G to whistle and reveal hidden bridges, traps, and secret clues.
  • Keep the car upright and stop before major crashes.
  • Each level adds a new trick, surface, or trap pattern.
